13Scheduled QOTD and AI-generated content

If enabled by a server owner, Scheduled QOTD may generate and publish questions automatically at the configured interval (every 1, 6, 12 or 24 hours), and the /random_qotd command may generate and publish a question immediately when invoked by an authorized member. Both use the server’s configured bot language and recent QOTD context. Question generation is provided through Groq, an independent third-party service. The server owner is responsible for deciding whether this feature is appropriate for their server and for avoiding the submission of confidential or sensitive content in QOTD history.

Automated generation may occasionally produce unsuitable, inaccurate, repetitive, or unexpected content. Quello does not guarantee that generated questions will be error-free or appropriate for every community. Server administrators should review their server’s settings and may disable Scheduled QOTD at any time.

If generation or publication fails, Quello retries every five minutes for up to one hour. After that period, it attempts to notify the server owner in English.
