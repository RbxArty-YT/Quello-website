01Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information Quello (“Quello”, “the Bot”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) may process when installed on or used within a Discord server. We aim to collect and retain only information reasonably necessary to provide the Bot’s features.

02Information We Process

Core Bot data

The Bot may process Discord User IDs, Server (Guild) IDs, Channel IDs, Message IDs, and QOTD IDs, including:

  • QOTD records — the question, creator, QOTD number, language, creation date and time, server ID, channel ID, message ID, and the ID of the related discussion thread.
  • Server configuration — role IDs, channel IDs, QOTD settings and counters, the bot interface language, the lock and maintenance states, the allowed role ID, the optional mention role ID, and the @everyone and @here mention settings.
  • Normal QOTD logs — action type, user ID, related QOTD ID, additional action details, and date and time. Changes made through /config are recorded here.
  • Audit log — interaction type (such as slash command, button, dropdown, or modal), command or component name, status, user ID, channel ID, additional details, and date and time. Audit records are kept separately from normal QOTD logs and are not removed by /reset_logs.
  • Interaction IDs — kept briefly to prevent duplicate actions.

Discussion threads

For messages posted in a QOTD discussion thread, Quello records the author’s User ID, the message ID, the channel ID, and the date and time so that answers can be counted for statistics and leaderboards and audited, including edits and deletions.

The text of those messages is not stored.

Question suggestions

When a member submits a question with /suggest, Quello stores:

  • the suggested question and its language;
  • the submitter’s User ID;
  • the suggestion’s status (pending, approved, rejected or deleted by its author);
  • the reason a moderator gives when rejecting it, and the reviewer’s User ID;
  • the date and time of submission and review.

Quello sends the submitter a direct message with the decision (the rejection reason, or a link to the published QOTD); if the member’s direct messages are closed, nothing is sent. If a suggestion is published, the QOTD record keeps the ID of the member credited as its suggester.

Statistics

Personal statistics shown by /me and /question_week are calculated from the records described above.

Website dashboard

If you sign in to the website dashboard with Discord, Quello receives your Discord User ID, display name, and avatar through Discord OAuth2 (the identify scope only; no email, password, messages, or server list). The Discord access token is used once to read this profile and is then revoked on a best-effort basis.

Your User ID is checked by the Bot to decide what you may do in each server, following the same rules as the equivalent commands:

  • the server owner (and the Bot’s developer) can view and change settings, use Maintenance and Lock, publish and edit QOTDs, review suggestions, and read the activity and audit logs;
  • members who hold the server’s allowed manager role can view settings, publish and edit QOTDs, and review suggestions.

Your profile details are kept in a signed session token stored in your browser until it expires (7 days by default) or you log out; they are not stored in the Bot database. The website also keeps your language and theme choices in your browser.

Changes made from the dashboard (including publishing, editing, approving or rejecting) are recorded in the normal QOTD logs and the audit log with your User ID and are marked as made from the web, like the same actions made with slash commands.

03How We Use Information

Information is used to provide QOTD functionality, history, statistics and leaderboards, normal QOTD logs, detailed interaction auditing, permission controls, server configuration, maintenance and lock features, troubleshooting, and protection against abuse or unauthorized use.

We do not sell or use this information for advertising.

04Message Content

Quello may process message content directly submitted through its commands when required to perform the requested action, such as creating or editing a QOTD. For example, a QOTD question may be stored for history or authorized editing. The Bot does not intentionally collect unrelated private messages for purposes unrelated to its functionality.

05Data Storage

Quello uses a database to store information required for its features. The current implementation uses SQLite, and the database is backed up automatically once a day. Access is limited to the developer and the Bot’s infrastructure as reasonably necessary to operate and maintain the service.

06Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent information processed by Quello. Information may be processed through Discord’s services and APIs as necessary for the Bot to operate. We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect security, investigate abuse or unauthorized access, or protect rights and safety.

07Data Retention

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Bot’s functionality. QOTD history and administrative logs may be retained longer when required for history, statistics, administrative, or security features.

  • Automatic database backups are rotated, and only the most recent seven daily backups are kept.
  • Interaction IDs used to prevent duplicate actions are removed after about 30 days.
  • Deleting a QOTD with /delete removes its record and its recorded answers from the active database.

08Data Deletion Requests

Users may contact the developer regarding information associated with their use of Quello. Server administrators may request deletion of data associated with their server. Requests may require sufficient information to identify the relevant data. Some information may be retained when necessary for security, legal, or legitimate operational purposes.

09Security

We use reasonable technical measures intended to protect stored information. However, no method of electronic storage or Internet transmission can guarantee absolute security.

10Third-Party Services

Quello operates through Discord and uses Discord’s API and services. Discord is a separate third-party service with its own policies. Quello does not control how Discord processes information within its platform.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and the dashboard API may be reached through a connectivity provider such as Cloudflare. These services can process technical request data (for example IP address, browser information and request times) as part of delivering the website and API, under their own policies. Quello does not use this technical data for advertising.

11Children’s Privacy

Quello does not intentionally request sensitive personal information. Users should not provide passwords, payment information, government identification documents, home addresses, or other sensitive information to the Bot.

12Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The Last Updated date will be changed when appropriate.

13Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, data processing, or deletion requests, please contact the developer through the Bot’s official support channel.
